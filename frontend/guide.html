Detailed Search

Filter cases by bench, judge, parties, and date range

1

Open Detailed Search

Click Detailed Search in the sidebar or from the home screen.

2

Select a Bench *

Choose the court bench from the dropdown. This is the only required field. All other filters are optional.

3

Narrow by Judge or Case Type (optional)

After selecting a bench, the Judge and Author Judge dropdowns populate. You can also filter by Coram, Case Type, Case Number, or Case Year.

4

Enter Party or Advocate Names (optional)

Fill in Petitioner Name, Respondent Name, or their advocate fields to narrow results to specific parties.

5

Search Multiple Entities at Once (optional)

Use the Search multiple entities box to enter several party names: one per line or comma-separated. Choose whether the names apply to the Petitioner or Respondent field. Each name is searched separately and all results are merged.

6

Set a Date Range

Enter a From and To date for the order date. Required unless Case Type, Number, and Year are all filled in. Ranges longer than 90 days are automatically split and merged.

7

Run the Search

Click ▶ Run Search. The CAPTCHA is solved automatically. Watch the Activity Log for live progress. When done, click Download Excel to save all results.

Set a custom filename in Save results as before running — the downloaded file will use that name.

Quick Search

Look up a single case by its exact reference

1

Open Quick Search

Click Quick Search in the sidebar or from the home screen.

2

Select a Bench *

Choose the bench where the case was filed.

3

Enter the Case Reference *

Select the Case Type, enter the Case Number, and choose the Case Year. All three fields are required.

4

Run the Search

Click ▶ Run Search. The CAPTCHA is handled automatically and the case record is fetched directly from the court website and displayed in the Results section below.

AI Assistant

Ask questions in plain language

1

Open the AI Assistant

Click AI Assistant in the sidebar or from the home screen.

2

Type Your Query

Describe what you're looking for in plain English, for example: "Find writ petitions filed by Nandeesh against the State of Karnataka in 2023" or "How do I search for cases from a specific judge?"

3

Send the Message

Press Enter or click the send button. Use Shift + Enter to start a new line without sending.

4

Follow the Guidance

The assistant will interpret your request and guide you to the right search tool, suggest which fields to fill in, or answer general questions about how the application works. Continue the conversation naturally.

The assistant remembers your conversation for the current session. You can ask follow-up questions without repeating context.
