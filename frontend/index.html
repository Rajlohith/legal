Karnataka High Court Case Research Tool

Retrieve and analyse case records from the Karnataka Judiciary portal automatically. Search by bench, judge, party names, date ranges, or case reference. Results are collated and ready to download in seconds.

Use the AI Assistant to describe what you need in plain language and let it guide you to the right search. Or go directly to Detailed Search for full filter control, or Quick Search when you have an exact case reference.

Search Tools

AI Assistant

Describe what you're looking for in plain language. The assistant interprets your query and guides you to the right fields, no form knowledge required.

Detailed Search

Full control, filter by bench, judge, parties, advocate names, date range, and more. Handles multi-entity searches and long date windows automatically.

Quick Search

Look up a specific case instantly using its exact Bench, Case Type, Case Number, and Case Year. Fastest path when you have the full reference.
